# Logistics Operations Dashboard

## Project Overview

The Logistics Operations Dashboard is a business intelligence and data analytics project designed to provide insights into the operational performance of a logistics company.

The dashboard brings together key operational metrics across revenue generation, fleet utilization, driver performance, fuel consumption, maintenance costs, customer performance, and safety incidents.

The goal is to transform operational data into meaningful insights that support data-driven decision-making, improve efficiency, reduce operational costs, and enhance overall business performance.

## Dashboard Preview

The dashboard is organized into eight major sections:

### 1. Overview
Provides a high-level summary of the company's operational performance, including:
- Total Revenue
- Estimated Profit
- Total Trips
- Fuel Cost
- On-Time Delivery Rate
- Revenue Trends

### 2. Revenue & Routes
Analyzes revenue generation across different routes.
- Top Routes by Revenue
- Fuel Cost by Route
- Monthly Revenue Trends
- Top Customers on Selected Routes

### 3. Driver Performance
Evaluates driver productivity and efficiency.
- Total Drivers
- Average Trips per Driver
- On-Time Delivery Performance
- Incidents per Driver
- Top Drivers by Revenue
- Safety Incidents by Driver

### 4. Fleet Performance
Monitors fleet utilization and performance.
- Total Trucks
- Active Trucks
- Truck Utilization
- Maintenance Costs
- Maintenance Cost per Truck
- Trips per Truck

### 5. Fuel & Maintenance
Examines fuel consumption and vehicle maintenance expenses.
- Total Fuel Cost
- Maintenance Cost
- Average Maintenance Cost
- Fuel Cost Trends
- Maintenance Cost Trends
- Fuel Cost by Route

### 6. Customer Performance
Provides insights into customer contribution and service delivery.
- Top Customers by Revenue
- Top Customers by Trips
- On-Time Delivery by Customer
- Revenue by Load Type

### 7. Safety Analysis
Examines operational safety and incident patterns.
- Total Safety Incidents
- Incidents per Driver
- Total Claim Amount
- Incidents by Type
- Claims by Incident Type
- Incidents by Driver
- Monthly Incident Trends

### 8. Operational Trends
Tracks changes in business performance over time.
- Revenue Trends
- Trip Volume Trends
- Fuel and Maintenance Cost Trends
- On-Time Delivery Trends

## Key Business Questions

This dashboard helps answer important business questions:

1. Which routes generate the highest revenue?
2. How efficiently are drivers performing?
3. What is the relationship between fleet utilization and operational performance?
4. How much is the company spending on fuel and vehicle maintenance?
5. Which customers contribute the most to revenue?
6. What patterns can be identified in safety incidents?
7. How are revenue and trip volumes changing over time?
8. How can operational costs be reduced while maintaining service quality?
